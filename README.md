## About Me

I’m drawn to problems where the answer isn’t simply in the data, but in understanding **what to measure, why it matters and what decision should follow**. My interests span marketing, product, growth and commercial analytics, with a growing focus on experimentation, predictive modelling and applied AI.

What I enjoy most is taking an open-ended business problem, making sense of the data behind it, and getting to an answer that is actually useful.
